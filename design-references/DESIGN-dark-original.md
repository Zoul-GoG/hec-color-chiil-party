## Brand & Style
The brand manifests an electrifying, nocturnal lounge celebration—merging the collegiate prestige of HEC Abidjan with the pulse of high-octane collaborative nightlife. Designed for tastemakers, social curators, and late-night revelers, the interface delivers a magnetic, ultra-modern experience where shared music curation feels kinetic, celebratory, and effortless.

The visual direction combines sleek dark-mode glassmorphism with high-vibe glowing accents. Deep midnight canvases serve as the stage, pierced by festive crimson/ruby energy, royal blue laser highlights, and crisp, translucent glass layers. Interfaces feel weightless yet punchy: tactile frosted panels reflect atmospheric neon light, sound waves visually reverberate through glowing rims, and every track addition feels like dropping a fresh beat into the center of a VIP lounge.

## Colors
The palette anchors in a velvety night backdrop, elevated by vivid crimson flares and electric royal blue currents.

- **Primary (`#E11D48`)**: The celebratory ruby core. Applied to active play states, hot-voting indicators, prime CTAs, and ambient focal glows.
- **Secondary (`#1D4ED8`)**: The deep royal club pulse. Drives live collaboration badges, active user rings, secondary audio controls, and foundational gradient underlays.
- **Tertiary (`#F43F5E`)**: High-energy rose neon used for active soundwave peaks, live track heatmaps, and pulsing indicators.
- **Neutral (`#0B1120`)**: Deep midnight navy forming the root surface canvas, keeping contrast razor-sharp without turning into pitch black.
- **Surface Tiers**:
  - `surface-canvas`: `#0B1120` (root canvas).
  - `surface-glass`: `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.65)` layered with backdrop blur.
  - `surface-elevated`: `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.75)` with subtle crimson/blue specular highlights.
- **Text & Content**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) commands headline prominence; muted blues and cool slates (`#94A3B8`) handle secondary metadata and timestamps.

## Typography
Typography channels the syncopated tempo of modern electronic beats and high-energy gatherings.

- **Headlines (`Sora`)**: Bold, structural, and subtly futuristic. Generous counterforms and rhythmic geometric curves capture a festive, forward-thinking presence across playlist banners, modal titles, and party stage screens.
- **Body & Labels (`Hanken Grotesk`)**: Crisp, balanced, and razor-sharp. Ensures zero friction during fast-paced party environments when tracking contributor votes, track lengths, queue sequences, and artist credits.
- **Hierarchy Rules**: Display headings make heavy use of tight negative tracking to feel punchy and compact. Labels and status chips employ slight positive tracking with upper or semibold weight for rapid, glanceable legibility across dim lighting.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a modular 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to transition seamlessly from one-handed mobile DJ mode to wide desktop lounge dashboard screens.

- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column structure with `margin: 1rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Track cards span full width; voting actions pin to the thumb-zone along the bottom glass dock.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: 8-column layout with `margin: 1.5rem` and `gutter: 1.25rem`. Two-column split featuring the active "Now Playing & Heatmap" on the left and the collaborative queue on the right.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12-column layout with `margin: 2.5rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`, capping at a max content container width of 1440px. Three-panel distribution: Session Contributors & Channels (3 cols), Collaborative Queue & Search (6 cols), Now Playing Visualizer & DJ Deck (3 cols).
- **Rhythm**: All paddings adhere strictly to the 4px baseline system (`0.25rem` multipliers) to maintain razor-sharp alignment amidst glass card borders.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created through luminous optical stacking and glassmorphic multi-planar surfaces rather than opaque skeuomorphism.

- **Plane 0 (Canvas Base)**: Deepest midnight tone `#0B1120`, occasionally underlaid with organic, slowly shifting radial gradients of `#1D4ED8` (opacity 15%) and `#E11D48` (opacity 12%).
- **Plane 1 (Container Glass)**: Background `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.65)` layered with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a subtle 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Plane 2 (Hover & Elevated Items)**: Background `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.70)` with a top border gradient highlighting down (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` to `transparent`). Ambient glow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.18)`.
- **Plane 3 (Modals, Overlays & Floats)**: Floating player bar and bottom sheets use `rgba(11, 17, 32, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`, framed by dual layered drop shadows: `0 16px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)` paired with a soft neon aura `0 0 24px rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.25)`.
- **Light Emitting Traces**: Interactive borders gain a directional crimson or royal blue edge when focused or playing, simulating internal fiber-optic edges.

## Shapes
The design embraces a refined, tight architectural silhouette (`roundedness: 1`). Core components employ a deliberate 0.25rem base radius (`rounded-sm`), delivering an authoritative, technical precision that contrasts gorgeously with atmospheric light glows.

- **Standard Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Small Chips)**: 0.25rem (`4px`). Retains a crisp, modern tech look.
- **Card Containers & Panels (`rounded-lg`)**: 0.5rem (`8px`). Softens large card clusters while keeping track lists neat and structural.
- **Floating Modals & Player Bar (`rounded-xl`)**: 0.75rem (`12px`). Defines self-contained floating interfaces.
- **Exceptions**: Round avatars, track album artwork thumbnails (subtle 4px radius), and audio scrubber knobs remain true circles (`50%`) to denote rotational and fluid musical attributes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Drop Beat / Add Track)**: Solid `#E11D48` fill with crisp white `Sora` semi-bold typography. Features an inner top-edge highlight (`1px inset rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`) and a pulsing crimson drop glow (`0 0 16px rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.45)`).
- **Secondary (Collaborate / Invite)**: Frosted midnight tint (`rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.15)`), bordered with a 1px royal blue stroke (`#1D4ED8`), white text, and a hover transition to `rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.35)`.
- **Ghost & Icon Buttons**: Transparent with hover states transitioning to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Active states ignite in `#E11D48`.

### Search Bar
- Semi-translucent pill-bracket housing (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.75)`) with a 1px stroke of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
- Left-aligned glowing royal blue search icon (`#3B82F6`). Placeholder text in muted slate (`#64748B`).
- On focus: Border illuminates into `#E11D48`, accompanied by an outer ambient halo `0 0 12px rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.25)`. A trailing keyboard shortcut badge (`⌘K`) sits neatly on the right.

### Music Track Cards
- **Structure**: Glass card (`0.5rem` radius) spanning track cover art (48x48px), title (`Sora` 14px bold), artist/collaborator avatar, energetic waveform visualizer, and a real-time collaborative upvote counter.
- **Default State**: Background `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.5)`, 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
- **Now Playing / Active**: Background transitions to a subtle crimson-to-navy linear wash (`linear-gradient(90deg, rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.2) 0%, rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.1) 100%)`) with an active animated ruby equalizer bars icon on the right edge.
- **Vote/Bump Action**: Compact numeric counter button with fire/up-arrow icon that triggers a bursting micro-particle glow when tapped.

### Chips & Badges
- **Live Badges**: Ultra-compact (`0.25rem` radius), uppercase `label-sm` with a pulsating 6px crimson dot indicator (`#E11D48`) and backdrop blur.
- **Genre & Vibe Filters**: Sleek dark capsules with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` fill. Active state shifts to `#1D4ED8` with crisp white text.

### Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Inputs**: Midnight base, border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`, text `#FFFFFF`. Focus transitions to dynamic `#E11D48` ring.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 0.25rem rounded squares. Unchecked has a hairline border; checked fills with `#E11D48` displaying a white checkmark.

### Collaboration Deck & Presence Stack
- Overlapping contributor avatars with colored status rings (Royal Blue for DJ host, Ruby Red for top curator).
- Live counter pill displaying active listener count with an ambient oscillating crimson breathing dot.
